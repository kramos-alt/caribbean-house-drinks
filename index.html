Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do you travel to my venue?

Yes — I'm based in New Jersey and travel across NJ and nearby areas for private events, weddings and celebrations.

Do you provide the alcohol?

I bring the full bar experience — tools, mixers, fresh garnishes and service. Alcohol is planned together based on your event and guest count.

How far in advance should I book?

The earlier the better — weekends and holidays fill up fast. Reach out and I'll check availability for your date.

Can you create a custom menu?

Absolutely. Every event gets its own signature cocktails, plus mocktails and non-alcoholic options for all your guests.

How do I book or get a quote?

Just text or WhatsApp me your date, location and guest count — I'll send a custom quote and menu ideas.
